== Exercise: Sign in and locate the dashboards

. Open *OpenShift Console* in the Showroom right pane, or use the console link in your service details.
. Sign in with your assigned OpenShift identity and select `{app_namespace}`. If *All Projects* reports restricted access, select your assigned project and retry.
. Open the Network and RAN dashboard links in separate browser tabs. If links are unavailable, use the Administrator perspective's *Networking → Routes*: open `hub-frontend` for Network and `hub-ran-frontend` for RAN.
. Complete OpenShift authentication for both dashboards.
. Open the AAP console from your service details and sign in with the supplied credentials. Keep it available to inspect automation jobs.
. Keep the OpenShift console available for pod logs and workload checks.

The dashboards include *Network* and *Telco ORAN* navigation.
If a link points to an unavailable hostname, use your assigned route rather than constructing a URL from a sample domain.

=== Verify

* Both authenticated dashboards load.
* You can sign in to the AAP console.

=== Troubleshooting

An authentication loop, forbidden response in your own project, or inaccessible route is an access/deployment issue.
Record the URL, namespace, and error before triggering incidents.
