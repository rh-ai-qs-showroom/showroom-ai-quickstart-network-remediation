== Exercise: Sign in and locate the dashboards

. Open *OpenShift Console* in the Showroom right pane, or use the console link in your service details.
. Sign in with your assigned OpenShift identity.
. Select your application project. If the console reports restricted access while showing *All Projects*, select your assigned project and retry.
. In the Administrator perspective, open *Networking → Routes*. Locate `hub-frontend` for Network and `hub-ran-frontend` for RAN, and open each route's location in a separate browser tab.
. Complete OpenShift authentication for both dashboards. Use the provided route links if your console perspective names menus differently.
. Keep the console available for scoped pod logs and readiness checks. The *Terminal* tab requires a provisioned terminal service; browser exercises use the OpenShift console.

The dashboards include *Network* and *Telco ORAN* navigation.
If a link points to an unavailable hostname, use your assigned route rather than constructing a URL from a sample domain.

=== Verify

* Both authenticated dashboards load for your assigned seat.
* You can select the assigned application and edge projects.
* You can open *Workloads → Pods*, select a pod, and view its *Logs*.
* You know that the current learner role is read-only. The mock-ticket lookup in Module 5 requires an authorized facilitator to use a pod terminal and share the resulting ticket fields.

=== Troubleshooting

An authentication loop, forbidden response in your own project, or inaccessible route is an access/deployment issue.
Capture the URL, namespace, and error for the facilitator before triggering incidents.
Opening a dashboard does not demonstrate that its AI dependencies are ready.
