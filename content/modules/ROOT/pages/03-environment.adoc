= Module 1: Explore the applications

Locate the services and dashboards introduced in xref:01-overview.adoc[workflow and architecture].

== Explore the services

. In the OpenShift console, select `{app_namespace}` and open *Workloads → Pods*. Locate the dashboard services, Network agent, RAN workflow services, and shared dependencies.
. Find the two predictor workloads, `ran-ml-service` and `ran-rca-ml-service`. Both run on CPU; the language model is accessed through the external Models-as-a-Service (MaaS) endpoint.
. Switch to `{edge_namespace}` and find the `edge-nginx` deployment. In *Workloads → ConfigMaps*, locate `adnr-ran-state`, which records actions against the RAN simulator.
. Open the *Network dashboard*. Locate *Demo Mode*, *Incident Timeline*, *Integration Status Matrix*, and *NOC Chat*.
. Open the *RAN dashboard*. Locate *Demo Mode*, *Anomaly List*, and *Recent Anomalies*.

=== Verify

* Both dashboards are open and you can locate their scenario controls.
* You can find both predictor workloads, the edge deployment, and the RAN simulator state.
