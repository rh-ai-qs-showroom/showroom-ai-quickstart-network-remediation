= Module 1: Explore the applications and check readiness
:page-aliases: 04-readiness.adoc

Locate the controls and check dependencies before triggering an incident.

== Exercise 1: Check the dashboards and predictors

. In the *Network dashboard*, locate *Demo Mode* and *Incident Timeline*. Open the *Integration Status Matrix* and check Kubernetes, Kafka, AAP, and ServiceNow. Ask *NOC Chat*: `MCP health status across all integrations`.
+
.Example Integration Status Matrix.
image::network-integration-matrix.png[Network Integration Status Matrix showing integration status and HTTP responses,link=self,window=blank]
+
. In the *RAN dashboard*, locate *Demo Mode*, *Anomaly List*, and *Recent Anomalies*. Check for dependency warnings.
. In the OpenShift console, select `{app_namespace}` and open *Workloads → Pods*. Check that the dashboard, Network agent, and RAN workflow containers are ready.
. Use console resource search to find *InferenceServices* (`serving.kserve.io/v1beta1`). Open `ran-ml-service` and `ran-rca-ml-service` and confirm that each has a `Ready` condition of `True`.
. If a required dependency is unavailable, record the error and ask the facilitator to resolve it before continuing.

[NOTE]
====
The LokiStack integration is not used in these exercises. A `Partial data` warning naming that dependency alone does not prevent you from continuing.
====

=== Verify

* You can locate the scenario controls, and required integrations are available.
* Workflow containers and both predictors are ready.

== Exercise 2: Locate automation and its targets

. In `{edge_namespace}`, find *Workloads → Deployments → edge-nginx*. Under *Workloads → ConfigMaps*, locate `adnr-ran-state`, which records RAN simulator actions.
. In the *AAP console*, locate the templates for `restart-nginx`, `scale-up-workers`, `ran-cell-recovery`, and `ran-load-balance`. Their names can include an environment-specific prefix.
. Open a template and locate its job history. Later, use each dashboard's job ID to find the matching execution.

Do not launch a job manually. The exercises trigger automation through the incident workflows.

=== Verify

* You can find the edge deployment, RAN simulator state, and AAP templates and job history.
