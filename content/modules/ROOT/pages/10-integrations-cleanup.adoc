= Module 8: Connect the demo to real users

Identify what an operations team needs to connect these patterns to real systems, and review who owns the tenant lifecycle.

== Learning objectives

* Map simulated integrations to real counterparts.
* Describe evidence needed to establish actual recovery.
* Explain Argo CD ownership and cleanup order.

== Exercise 1: Design an operational handoff

. Choose one recorded Network incident and one RAN incident. Summarize observations, model decisions, retrieved evidence, and mock results.
. For each simulated boundary, describe the real source or destination and how you would verify the outcome.
. Identify the human responder and approval boundary for automated changes. Keep Network ticket escalation separate from RAN classification and remediation.

== Exercise 2: Review deployment and cleanup ownership

[IMPORTANT]
====
Installation, reset, and cleanup are pending fresh-environment validation against tenant GitOps packaging.
This exercise reviews the lifecycle.
Use the facilitator's validated environment procedure when it becomes available.
====

. In GitOps, review the four tenant applications if access is available. Bootstrap Helm creates Argo CD Applications; Argo CD renders application charts and owns their resources. Bootstrap install completion alone does not prove readiness.
. Review `-hub` and `-edge` destinations. They must resolve to your two namespaces on the same cluster; `-predictors` must also target your application namespace.
. Discuss cleanup order: remove the tenant application bootstrap release, wait for Argo CD cascading deletion of all four applications and their resources, remove the tenant platform/AppProject, then remove tenant namespace infrastructure.
. Identify data lost at the final step: both namespaces, remaining persistent volume claims, retained secrets, and injected tenant credentials. Provision a fresh seat for a complete reset.
. Confirm that shared operators, the GitOps controller, and other tenants remain platform-owned.

The current package disables automatic self-healing and pruning by default for demonstrations.
Selected edge workload changes are excluded from reconciliation.
A sync does not necessarily reset incident history, reverse edge changes, or recreate a clean seat.
RAN's *Clear* removes displayed anomaly history; it is not a deployment reset.
*Reset Lightspeed Demo* is outside this profile.

=== Verify

* You can explain why the controller and AppProject must remain until cascading deletion finishes.
* You distinguish clearing a view, reconciling Git, and deleting a seat.
* You have saved evidence notes before facilitator-managed teardown.
