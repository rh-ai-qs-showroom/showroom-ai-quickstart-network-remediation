= Module 2: Check application readiness

Check that the dashboards, models, and automation are available before triggering an incident.

== Learning objectives

* Check the Network and RAN dashboards for dependency failures.
* Locate the two predictors and the automation templates used in the exercises.

== Exercise 1: Check the dashboards and predictors

. Open Network's *Integration Status Matrix*. Check the Kubernetes, Kafka, AAP, and ServiceNow integrations. Ask *NOC Chat*: `MCP health status across all integrations`.
. Open RAN and check for dependency warnings.
. In the OpenShift console, select `{app_namespace}` and open *Workloads → Pods*. Check that the dashboard and workflow containers are ready.
. Use console resource search to find *InferenceServices* (`serving.kserve.io/v1beta1`). Open `ran-ml-service` and `ran-rca-ml-service` and confirm that each has a `Ready` condition of `True`.
. If a required dependency is unavailable, share the displayed error with the facilitator before continuing.

=== Verify

* Both dashboards respond and required integrations are available.
* Both predictors are ready.

[NOTE]
====
The LokiStack integration is not used in these exercises. A `Partial data` warning naming that dependency alone does not prevent you from continuing.
====

== Exercise 2: Check automation access

. Open the *AAP console* using your service details.
. Locate the templates for `restart-nginx`, `scale-up-workers`, `ran-cell-recovery`, and `ran-load-balance`. The displayed names can include an environment-specific prefix.
. Open a template and locate its job history. You will use the job ID from each dashboard to find the matching execution after triggering a scenario.
. Ask the facilitator to confirm that Ansible Lightspeed and its playbook-generation path are ready for Module 4, and that both the Network runbooks and RAN documents are available for retrieval.

Do not launch a job manually. The exercises trigger automation through the incident workflows.

=== Verify

* You can view the templates and job history in AAP.
* Lightspeed and the document collections are ready for the exercises.
